# Periscope

A workspace to help design leaders see projects, people, priorities and capacity.

**[Start here: what matters now →](now.md)**

Have something useful to add? Share a link and a sentence about what changed, what you noticed, or where you need help. You can edit the page or give it to the agent to fold in. Rough notes are fine.

The agent keeps the summary short and gathers activity from the repositories. People contribute judgment, findings and decisions as part of their work.
