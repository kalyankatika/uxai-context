# Explore relationship-based discovery

> **Reference example only — not an actual project record.**

## Intent

Explore whether visible relationships help people discover relevant work faster than a traditional list or folder structure.

## Why

Current information is easy to find when someone already knows what it is called, but harder to discover when entering through a topic, person, or adjacent project.

## Current thinking

A lightweight relationship view may provide better orientation without requiring a heavy knowledge-graph experience.

The first prototype should stay simple and test the usefulness of the model before adding more relationship types.
