# People look for related work through familiar concepts

> **Reference example only — not an actual project record.**

## Question

How do people naturally look for related work when they do not already know the project name?

## Evidence

Across several conversations, participants started with familiar concepts such as a capability, customer problem, or teammate rather than a formal project name.

Participants also wanted to understand why two items were connected before opening additional detail.

## Finding

Project name alone is a weak discovery entry point for people who are not already close to the work.

Concepts and explicit relationships provide stronger orientation.

## Implication

Future exploration should support multiple paths into the same context and make relationships understandable at a glance.
