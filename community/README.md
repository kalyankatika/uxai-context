# UX AI Community

This area captures the lightweight principles for how community activity can connect to `uxai-context`.

It is intentionally **not** a fully operational governance system yet.

Our immediate focus is:

1. establish the broader UX AI Context pattern
2. use Periscope to prove the day-to-day project workflow
3. learn from real participation before formalizing community mechanics

The community layer can evolve as Teams, Viva Engage, email, demos, office hours, GitHub, and other engagement channels come online.

## The role of community channels

Community channels are good for:

- discovery
- conversation
- questions
- demos
- invitations to participate
- sharing work in progress
- connecting people with similar interests

They should remain open and low-friction.

The repo plays a different role:

> **Community channels create energy. UX AI Context preserves what deserves to compound.**

A healthy loop is:

```text
share → discuss → try/build/learn → capture what matters → reuse → share again
```

## Principles we want to preserve

- **Share broadly. Promote selectively.**
- **Promote signal, not activity.**
- **Capture the reusable part, not everything.**
- **Participation can be broad. Canonical context should be curated.**
- **Experimentation should not require approval.**
- **Durable/shared context should have enough depth to be trusted and reused.**

## Captured areas

These subdirectories preserve the thinking we have already established:

- [Engagement](engagement/) — how community channels connect to durable context
- [Contributions](contributions/) — what makes something worth preserving or promoting
- [Governance](governance/) — lightweight stewardship of canonical/shared context

These are reference principles for now, not mandatory process.

When community participation is active enough to justify a more formal operating model, we can develop these areas based on real usage rather than assumptions.
